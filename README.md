# Brand New Day Experience

Build a premium cinematic interactive movie promotional website for "Brand New Day".

Use the uploaded video as the central storytelling element.

The website should not feel like a normal landing page. It should feel like an interactive movie experience where the user discovers the story while scrolling.

As the user scrolls, the uploaded video should stay pinned and scrub smoothly with the scroll. Every scene should reveal new story elements synchronized with the video.

STORYTELLING EXPERIENCE

Opening Scene

- The screen is almost dark.

- A subtle city skyline appears with rain, fog and cinematic ambience.

- The movie title "BRAND NEW DAY" slowly reveals with dramatic lighting.

- A tagline fades in:

  "Every ending is the beginning of something greater."

Scene 2 – The World

- The video continues.

- Large cinematic text appears one sentence at a time.

- Explain that the city has changed after a mysterious event.

- The streets are filled with uncertainty.

- Hope is disappearing.

Scene 3 – Newspaper Reveal

- A newspaper unfolds from the left side.

- It contains animated breaking news headlines.

- Headlines update as the user scrolls.

- The newspaper feels alive with paper folds, ink reveal and comic-book inspired styling.

Example headlines:

• BREAKING NEWS

• A New Hero Emerges

• The City Faces Its Greatest Challenge

• Mysterious Events Continue

• Hope Returns

Scene 4 – The Hero

- Introduce the mysterious hero.

- Do not reveal everything immediately.

- Reveal the story through cinematic text animations.

- Use dramatic typography and lighting.

Scene 5 – Villain Threat

- The atmosphere becomes darker.

- Add smoke, particles and cinematic lighting.

- Explain that a dangerous new enemy threatens the city.

- Create suspense instead of revealing everything.

Scene 6 – Character Showcase

- Interactive character cards.

- 3D hover effects.

- Animated borders.

- Floating glass panels.

Scene 7 – Timeline

- Show the events leading to the final battle.

- Animated timeline with parallax transitions.

Scene 8 – Trailer

- Large cinematic trailer section.

- Animated play button.

Scene 9 – Gallery

- Movie stills with hover animations.

- Cinematic lightbox.

Scene 10 – Countdown

- Live countdown until release.

Scene 11 – Final Scene

- The city lights return.

- Final cinematic quote appears.

- End with the movie logo and release date.

VISUAL STYLE

- Sony Pictures quality presentation

- Hollywood blockbuster feel

- Dark cinematic UI

- Black, Red and Blue palette

- Glassmorphism

- Film grain

- Vignette

- Smoke

- Fog

- Dynamic lighting

- Floating particles

- Premium typography

ANIMATIONS

- GSAP

- ScrollTrigger

- Lenis Smooth Scroll

- SplitType text animations

- Three.js particles

- Mouse parallax

- Magnetic buttons

- Clip-path reveals

- Paper unfolding animation

- Comic-inspired transitions

The final website should feel like an official Hollywood interactive promotional website where users experience the story while scrolling instead of simply reading it.       Designed & Developed by TECHICS
